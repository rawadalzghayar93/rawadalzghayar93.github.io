Next Mission Uploader

Privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Next Mission Uploader ("the app") is a private tool run by the owner of the Next Mission YouTube channel. It connects to that one channel through the YouTube API Services. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to manage the owner's Y ouTube account. With that permission it:

  • uploads videos the owner produced to the Next Mission channel;
  • sets and edits titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and privacy for those videos;
  • reads channel and video statistics (views, likes, comments, subscriber count) for the channel 's own videos;
  • searches public YouTube videos to find Creative Commons sources.

The app does not access any other person's YouTube account, Google account, contacts, email or f iles.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish the channel's own videos and to measure how they perform. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else, except as needed to sen d requests to YouTube itself.

Where the data is stored

The sign-in token and a small log of uploaded videos and their statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server operated by the app. The owner can delet e these files at any time.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer reach t he channel. The stored token stops working and can be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
